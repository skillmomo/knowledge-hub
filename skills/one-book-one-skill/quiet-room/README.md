# Quiet Room (静屋)

[中文](#中文) | [English](#english)

## 中文

给 AI 用的小屋改造 skill。*一书一 skill · 06*

作者：skill整理员

### 它做什么

屋子让人累，多半不是因为小，而是因为太亮、太满、太花、路被挡着、眼睛没地方歇。

AI 默认爱「加东西」：一堆收纳盒、一盏更亮的灯、几样网红装饰。静屋反过来：**先减、先排、再加**。不换房子、不砸墙、少花钱，先改「人待在里面的感觉」。它让 AI 先看五件事：

- **光**：用光影，不用一样亮。分清任务光、环境光、重点光；晚上关掉顶灯，开两三盏低处的暖灯。
- **收纳**：分类 → 选址 → 定尺寸 → 协调。先看有哪些东西、在哪用，再决定放哪，不先买大柜子。
- **动线**：把每天最常走的两三条路空出来，让屋子给的提示和人想走的一样。
- **颜色和材料**：一起定。一个主色、一个辅助色、最多一种点缀色，露出木、布、纸的纹理。
- **借景**：学中国园林「小中见大」，把窗外的树、天色借进视线；窗框当画框，窗前不堆东西。

回答有固定步骤：先看照片或问三个问题 → 五件事各给一条改法 → 先写不花钱的，再写要买的 → 描述「改后会怎样」→ 需要时写「改造前 / 改造后」同机位效果图提示词。最后有一份自检清单。

### 什么时候用

出租屋、卧室、小户型、书房、工作室、宿舍的布置；读一张房间照片给改法；给 3D 或效果图写提示词；给家具采购排先后。常见说法：「屋子乱」「待着累」「房间压抑」「小屋怎么布置」「出租屋改造」「灯光怎么弄」「收纳」「动线」「配色」「静屋」。

### 怎么用

把 `quiet-room` 文件夹复制到你的 skills 目录（Claude Code 是 `~/.claude/skills/`）。skill 文件（`SKILL.md`）是中文写的。

### 来源

据《室内设计基础》（和田浩一、富坚优子、小川由佳利，中文 2014 年版）和《居所的匠心：中国居住文化》（陈平）的读书笔记整理。skill 是原创整理，不引原话。书里的例子多来自日本，中国出租屋的部分是引申，不是作者原话。静屋让屋子更清楚、更好待，不保证「治愈焦虑」。

## English

A small-room makeover skill for AI. *One Book, One Skill (一书一 skill) · 06*

Author: skill整理员

### What it does

A room is tiring less because it is small and more because it is too bright, too full, too busy, the path is blocked, and the eye has nowhere to rest.

By default, AI likes to *add*: more storage boxes, a brighter lamp, a few trendy decorations. Quiet Room does the opposite: **remove first, arrange next, add last**. No moving, no knocking down walls, little money. It changes how the room *feels* by making the AI look at five things first:

- **Light**: light and shadow, not even brightness. Separate task, ambient, and accent light; at night, turn off the ceiling light and use two or three low, warm lamps.
- **Storage**: sort, place, size, coordinate. Start from the real things and where they are used, not from a big new cabinet.
- **Path**: clear the two or three routes walked every day, so the room's cues match where people want to go.
- **Color and material**: decided together. One main color, one supporting color, at most one accent; let wood, fabric, and paper show their texture.
- **Borrowed view**: from Chinese gardens, "the large within the small". Bring the trees or the sky outside into view; treat the window frame as a picture frame and keep it clear.

Answers follow fixed steps: read the photo or ask three questions → one change for each of the five things → free changes first, purchases second → describe the room "after" → if needed, write matching "before / after" image prompts from the same camera position. It ends with a self-check list.

### When to use it

Rentals, bedrooms, small apartments, studies, studios, and dorm rooms; reading a room photo and suggesting changes; writing prompts for 3D or rendered views; ordering furniture purchases. Typical requests mention "messy room", "my room feels tiring", "small room layout", "rental makeover", "lighting", "storage", "traffic flow", "color scheme", or "Quiet Room".

### How to use

Copy the `quiet-room` folder into your skills directory (for Claude Code: `~/.claude/skills/`). The skill file (`SKILL.md`) is written in Chinese.

### Source

Based on reading notes from the Chinese editions of *Basics of Interior Design* (《室内设计基础》, by 和田浩一, 富坚优子, and 小川由佳利; Chinese edition 2014) and Chen Ping's 《居所的匠心：中国居住文化》 (on the craft of Chinese dwellings). The skill is an original summary and contains no quotations from the books. Most examples in the book are Japanese; notes on Chinese rentals are extensions, not the authors' words. Quiet Room aims to make a room clearer and easier to live in; it does not promise to cure anxiety.
