# A Good Table (一桌菜)

[中文](#中文) | [English](#english)

## 中文

给 AI 用的配菜 skill。*一书一skill · 14*

作者：skill整理员

### 它做什么

帮你配好一顿饭——不教做法，只管「这一顿吃哪几道、怎么搭、按什么顺序上」：

- 一道拿手菜当主角
- 浓的旁边，配一口清的
- 一桌菜每道一个味：甜、咸鲜、酸辣、清口、汤
- 菜数够吃就好（常用起点：人数 + 1）
- 上菜有先后：人等菜，别让菜等人

### 什么时候用

- 请朋友来家吃饭，不知道做什么
- 一个人的晚饭、给爸妈做一顿、年夜饭、生日饭
- 上次做的菜都是大家爱吃的，却没人吃多少，想找原因

### 怎么用

把 `SKILL.md` 放进你的 AI 工具的 skills 文件夹（例如 Claude Code 的 `~/.claude/skills/a-good-table/`），然后直接问：「朋友要来家里，做什么菜好？」「我上次做了这些菜，为什么没人吃？」

### 来源

袁枚《随园食单》（清），主要用「须知单」「戒单」里讲配菜、上菜、请客的几条。原文可在维基文库查到。skill 里加「」的是原书短句，其余是据书整理的要点和现代引申。

## English

A menu-planning skill for AI. *One Book, One Skill · 14*

Author: skill整理员

### What it does

Helps you plan a meal — not how to cook each dish, but which dishes to serve together and in what order:

- One dish you cook best is the star
- Next to something rich, put something fresh
- One flavor per dish: sweet, savory, sour-spicy, fresh, soup
- Just enough dishes (a common start: number of people + 1)
- Serve in order: guests wait for the food, not the food for the guests

### When to use

- Friends are coming over and you don't know what to cook
- A dinner alone, a meal for your parents, a New Year's or birthday dinner
- Last time every dish was a favorite, yet nobody ate much — and you want to know why

### How to use

Put `SKILL.md` in your AI tool's skills folder (for Claude Code: `~/.claude/skills/a-good-table/`), then just ask: "Friends are coming over — what should I cook?" or "Here's what I made last time. Why didn't anyone eat?"

### Source

Yuan Mei, *Recipes from the Garden of Contentment* (《随园食单》, Qing dynasty), mainly the chapters on what a cook must know and what to avoid. The original text is on Wikisource. In the skill, only text in 「」 is quoted from the book; the rest is summary and modern application. The skill itself is written in Chinese.
