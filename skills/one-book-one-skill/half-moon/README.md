# Half Moon (半月)

A Japanese-style aesthetics skill for AI. *One Book, One Skill (一书一 skill) · 01*

Author: skill整理员

## What it does

Half Moon teaches an AI how to *see* before it designs. It turns three classical aesthetic ideas into steps an AI can follow:

- **Yūgen (幽玄)**: depth. Show only half, and let the viewer complete the rest.
- **Mono no aware (物哀)**: transience. Capture the moment just before something disappears.
- **Sabi (寂)**: time and essence. As the surface fades, the true form shows.

It also covers two ways of looking (haiku's "second glance", and Bashō's school's *fueki ryūkō*, the unchanging and the ever-new), a five-step workflow (see, hide, fade, age, leave blank), a self-check list, palettes, typography notes, and templates.

## When to use it

Posters, slides, book covers, packaging, name cards, and wallpapers; rooms and shop or exhibition spaces; titles, copy, and short poems. Use it whenever you want work that is quieter, more restrained, and more lingering. Typical requests mention "Japanese style", "wabi-sabi", "yugen", "empty space", or "Half Moon".

## How to use

Copy the `half-moon` folder into your skills directory (for Claude Code: `~/.claude/skills/`). The skill file (`SKILL.md`) is written in Chinese.

## Source

Based on reading notes from the Chinese edition of Onishi Yoshinori's aesthetics trilogy (《日本美学三部曲》), which covers yūgen, aware, and sabi. Lineage named in the book: Fujiwara no Shunzei, Fujiwara no Teika, Zeami, Sen no Rikyū, Matsuo Bashō, and Motoori Norinaga. The skill is an original summary. Quotations are short and cited. Onishi's mapping of these ideas to beauty, the sublime, and humor is his own thesis, not a scholarly consensus.
